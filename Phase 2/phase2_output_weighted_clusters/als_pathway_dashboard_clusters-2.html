Key Findings

🟠 Threshold Effect (Binary Disruption)

🔴 Distinct Subtypes

Figure 4.3: Pathway Correlation Matrices

Spearman and Pearson correlations between pathway counts. Pearson is preferred for detecting linear, dose-dependent relationships.

Correlation Strength Tiers

Strong (|r| ≥ 0.7): Linear dose-dependent relationship — as one pathway intensifies, the other scales proportionally

Moderate (0.3 ≤ |r| < 0.7): Meaningful association but with variability

Weak (|r| < 0.3): Minimal linear relationship — pathways may co-occur but intensities are independent
